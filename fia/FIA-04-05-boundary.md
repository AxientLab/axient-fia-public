# FIA-04 / FIA-05 boundary

FIA-04 is the partial-to-final settlement assertion. FIA-05 is the subsequent
residual/collateral-release assertion. Their identity and causal relationship
are preserved in the selected P04→P05-v3 provenance edge, but they remain
separate parent FIA records with separate correlations and seven-position
derivation registries. This prevents a settlement record from being promoted
into a release record, or vice versa.
