| Synthetic data, scenario metadata, public evidence outputs, and publication-facing documentation | [CC BY 4.0](LICENSES/CC-BY-4.0.txt) | `evidence/`, `fia/`, `papers/`, `provenance/`, `benchmark/` |
| Schemas, canonicalizers, validators, and reference code | [Apache-2.0](LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt) | `schemas/`, `verifier/` |
| Axient names and marks | Not licensed | [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md) |

The directory-level declarations below apply unless a file carries a more
specific third-party licence notice. No third-party licence is altered by this
policy.

The supplied publication PDFs are author-provided canonical publication inputs.
They may be copied and verified in this companion as public outputs under the
CC BY 4.0 designation above; their scholarly attribution remains intact.
